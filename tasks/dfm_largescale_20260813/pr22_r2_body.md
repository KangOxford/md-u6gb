**Problem**

${\color{orange}\textsf{The B7b comment above closed Line A and located the compound-error driver in the sampler, not the representation. But the level penalty itself - draft 0.023 against corrected 0.411 at m=40 - was never causally decomposed. Four things in this PR's own corrector were never examined:}}$

${\color{orange}\textsf{1. Train/inference distribution mismatch. The corrector is trained on corrupt(real) to real, and deployed on corrupt(draft). It has never seen a draft during training, so nothing in its posterior can encode where drafts drift - the model scores its own draft tokens as high probability by construction.}}$

${\color{orange}\textsf{2. Residual architecture. The correction is LayerNorm(h-embed at P) added to the trunk output. LayerNorm renormalises any nonzero direction to unit variance, so the model cannot turn the correction down at positions where the draft is already right (34-47 percent of tokens agree with the real continuation); h-embed is the raw token embedding before any SSM layer, so the correction head sees no context and its ceiling is a per-token marginal adjustment; and the production path builds the model with t-cond off, so the corrector does not know which noise level it is operating at.}}$

${\color{orange}\textsf{3. Causal weights inside a symmetric mask. bidirectional=True replaces the causal SSD decay with W(l,s) = exp(-abs(s-l)) while the trunk parameters remain the frozen causal-pretrained ones. trunk-l1-shift = 0 verifies parameter identity; nothing verifies behaviour. The P=0 control that would separate "what the bidirectional pass does" from "what the learned residual does" exists as a flag (random-p-scale 0.0) and was never run.}}$

${\color{orange}\textsf{4. The keep-tau sweep - the one inference-side mechanism that could have limited edits - produced rollouts for 9 configurations that were never scored against the registered bar.}}$

**Plan**

1. Score the existing keep-tau sweep (tau in 0, 0.01, 0.1, 0.3; keep-field and keep-token variants) against the bar frozen in `run_tau_sweep.sh`: some tau reaches >= 3/5 fields with level below draft and slope <= 0.
2. Run the P=0 pure-bidirectional cell (`--random-p --random-p-scale 0.0`), same 27-ticker pool and n-seq 32 as the B7b cells, to decompose the corrector's output into structure effect vs learned correction.
3. R2, the distribution repair: dump a corpus of (draft tokens, real tokens, draft books) with a new `--dump-draft-only` runner mode over four 2025 training months, then retrain the residual with the corruption base decoupled from the CE target - corrupt the *draft*, reconstruct the *real* - so p0 becomes the distribution inference actually starts from. Branch `feat/dfm-r2-draft-corpus-20260830`; the AR gate and held-out eval stay on corrupt(real) so every number remains comparable with earlier arms.
4. Acceptance unchanged and frozen as registered: development on 2026-01, acceptance on 2026-02, level and slope reported apart per field, n-seq 32, each sampling seed against its own draft.

**Results** (tau sweep, P=0 cell and corpus pipeline final; R2 training numbers will be appended when they land)

${\color{blue}\textsf{Tau sweep, scored: all 9 configurations fail at 0/5. The structure is a cliff, not a trade-off curve: at tau = 0 the slope repair is real (price-rel slope -0.04 against draft +0.22) and the level is catastrophic (size +0.94 against draft +0.23); at any tau >= 0.01 the level returns to the draft and the slope advantage vanishes simultaneously (price-rel slope +0.224 against draft +0.221). No sweet spot exists between them. Mechanism: the draft is the model's own sample, so its confidence cannot mark drift - keep-tau can only find syntax-level errors. Any inference-side edit-limiting scheme keyed on the current model's confidence is dead for the same reason; the signal has to be put there by training, which is what R2 does.}}$

${\color{blue}\textsf{P=0 pure-bidirectional cell, final (25 tickers, 2026-01, n-seq 32): the decomposition is sharper than expected. With the residual zeroed the corrector still rewrites 62.6 percent of tokens and drags agreement with the real continuation from 0.513 to 0.335 - the destruction belongs to the mechanism, not to P. And the levels are catastrophic: event-type +1.284 against the learned corrector's +0.185 and the draft's +0.020; direction +0.423 against -0.017 and +0.012. So the untrained bidirectional pass is the dominant source of the level penalty, and most of what P learned is compensation for that self-inflicted damage (+1.28 down to +0.19) - still an order of magnitude above the draft after repair. The slope repair, by contrast, is genuinely learned: the P=0 cell has no negative slope anywhere (+0.02 to +0.12), while the learned corrector reaches price-rel slope -0.044. Consequence for the fix: the per-layer calibration of the bidirectional pass (or replacing the symmetric mask with two causal passes) moves from suggested to required - retraining on drafts alone would still spend capacity filling this hole.}}$

Four training months (2025-03/06/09/12, about 7,700 windows) generating now. Code: dfm-correct-runner --dump-draft-only (corpus), dfm-train-worker xbase decoupling (corrupt the draft, reconstruct the real), pushed on feat/dfm-r2-draft-corpus-20260830 at 19c74b9.}}$

${\color{blue}\textsf{R2 first cell, training underway (2026-08-31): same architecture and hyperparameters as the B7b on-cell (offset embedding, t-cond off, lr 1e-4, 8000 steps, field metric) with exactly one variable changed - the corruption base is the draft corpus instead of the real shard. AR gate passed at 0.758; the corpus branch is active in the log; steady state 0.5-0.7 s per step. One early observation reported as-is: at step 1 the held-out loss under the OLD corrupt-real objective jumps to 8.16 (the corrupt-real and corrupt-draft objectives pull the residual in different directions early on), which is itself evidence of how far apart the two input distributions are. The cell's registered acceptance is generation-side level and slope against the draft, not the old held-out; numbers follow when the 8000-step checkpoint rolls out.}}$

${\color{blue}\textsf{P=0 cell, second seed: replicated field by field (event-type +1.232 vs +1.284, direction +0.413 vs +0.423, all five same sign and magnitude, slopes all positive) - the decomposition now stands on two independent training seeds.}}$

${\color{blue}\textsf{R2 first round, four cells (offset-table on/off x seeds 42/43, same corpus snapshot, 8000 steps, same hyperparameters as B7b), scored on 2026-01: all four fail at 0/5, and the failure mode is itself the diagnosis confirmed. Three new pathologies: (i) timestamp overshoot - the R2 corrector drives dt-positive to 97.7-99.3 percent against real's 72 (B7b overshot by +11 points, R2 by +26): the corpus signal "real time moves forward" arrives, and an architecture with no amplitude control can only push it at full strength; (ii) inter-place anti-correlation deepens (zero-excluded hi-mid z reaches -100 to -146 in the no-table cells) - where the old corrector destroyed structure toward independence (+8), R2 pushes past it into anti-correlation; (iii) the blow-up field changes with the seed (price-rel +1.01 on seed 42, size +1.53 on seed 43) - high variance, not stable improvement. Two genuine positives survive: price-rel slope turns negative in 2 of 4 cells (-0.11, -0.21; the slope repair is reachable from the draft distribution), and the offset table still buys 4-5x on event-type under the new distribution - representation repair and distribution repair are orthogonal and both needed. Verdict: distribution repair alone is not the fix; the training signal reaches the model (behaviour changes drastically) and is then distorted by the same three architectural constraints the audits named (LayerNorm kills amplitude control, the residual sees no context, the bidirectional pass is off-distribution). The second round - same corpus, with the gated residual, per-layer bidirectional calibration and t-conditioning - moves from planned to required, and is next.}}$
