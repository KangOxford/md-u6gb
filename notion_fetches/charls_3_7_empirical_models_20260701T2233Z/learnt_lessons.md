## Empirical model interpretation

- A significant mediator-to-`Q_equal_fixed` closure coefficient is not enough to claim a complete mechanism; the cash-flow first stage also needs to be significant.
- In this run, pension has first-stage support for CES-D and life satisfaction, but not for loneliness.
- `low_transfer_zero` is mechanically related to `transfer`; the interaction model should not casually include both `ln_transfer` and the low-transfer dummy without explaining the overlap.
- Heterogeneity terms can be directionally interpretable in subgroup tables while the pooled interaction remains statistically insignificant; the interaction is the correct test for the 3.7.4 claim.
